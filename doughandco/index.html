Dough & Co is a premium bakery specializing in artisanal baked goods. We craft delicious butter biscuits, sugar cookies, tea cakes, and gift hampers using only the finest ingredients.

Premium Artisanal Baked Goods in Gauteng

Dough & Co is a trusted supplier of premium artisanal baked goods, supplying butter biscuits, sugar cookies, almond rocca, tea cakes, traditional rusks, and curated gift hampers to customers across Gauteng. We specialize in custom cakes and bulk orders, delivering reliability and value to families, event planners, and corporate clients.

Our Product Categories

  • Butter Biscuits: Premium flake, nutty flake, mint aero, and assorted butter biscuits.
  • Sugar Cookies: Customizable DIY cookies, iced sugar cookies, and festive holiday cookies.
  • Almond Rocca: Classic, dark chocolate, white chocolate, and mini almond rocca clusters.
  • Tea Cakes & Rusks: Moist fruit tea cakes, chocolate marble, and traditional buttermilk rusks.
  • Gift Hampers: Curated boxes perfect for corporate gifting, holidays, or treating someone special.

Why Choose Dough & Co?

  • Premium Quality: Crafted with love using only the finest ingredients.
  • Customization: DIY cookie options and custom cake consultations available.
  • Fast Delivery: Collection and delivery available across Gauteng.
  • Easy Ordering: Order via WhatsApp, phone, email, or our online shopping platform.
